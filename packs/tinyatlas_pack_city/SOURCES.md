# Texture sources

Every texture here is a scaled-down colour map (512 px). Ground textures as in OSM2World-default-style's selection; building textures turned grey and evened out so each building's colour can tint them (brick keeps its colour). Licences: [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/) CC0, [OSM Wiki Texture Library](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Texture_Library) public domain.
